Roster format

Keepers (optional)

Pre-assign a keeper to a team before creating the room (or starting a mock draft). The player comes off the board, fills a roster spot, and that team's pick in the round you choose is removed from the live draft. You can still add more from the lobby later.

Pick restrictions (optional)

Block a team from picking in a specific round before creating the room (or starting a mock draft) — for a forfeited pick, a punishment, or any house rule. That slot is skipped entirely in the live draft. You can still add more from the lobby later.

Custom stats (optional)

Add your own stat columns — advanced metrics, league-specific notes, whatever you want tracked — before creating the room (or starting a mock draft). Once added, fill in a value for any player from that player's detail popup in the draft room.

Mock drafts run entirely on this device — no invite link needed. Every other team drafts for itself (best player available) so you can practice instantly and re-run it as many times as you want.

Draft order

Reorder teams however you like — shuffle it, drag a team (⠿) to a new spot, or nudge one up/down with the arrows. Round 1 goes top to bottom, then it snakes back. Changes apply starting from the next pick; picks already made won't move.

Keepers

Assign a keeper to a team before the draft starts. The player comes off the board, fills a roster spot, and that team's pick in the round you choose is removed from the live draft.

🔒 Keepers are locked once the draft has started. Reset picks in the draft room to make changes.

Pick restrictions

Block a team from picking in a specific round — for a forfeited pick, a punishment, or any house rule. That slot is skipped entirely in the live draft.

🔒 Pick restrictions are locked once the draft has started. Reset picks in the draft room to make changes.

Add your own stat columns — advanced metrics, league-specific notes, whatever you want tracked. Once added, the commissioner can fill in a value for any player from that player's detail popup in the draft room.
